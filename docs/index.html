How Photo Sync uses your Google data

Photo Sync requests access to your Google Photos library solely to provide its core functionality. We access the minimum data necessary and never download, store, or share your photos.

  • Read your Google Photos library We read photo metadata — filenames and capture dates — to match photos between your Apple Photos and Google Photos libraries. We never download your actual photo or video content.
  • Archive photos in Google Photos When a photo is deleted from Apple Photos, we archive the matching photo in Google Photos. Archiving removes it from the main view but keeps it safely in your library. We do not permanently delete any photos.
